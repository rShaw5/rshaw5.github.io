robin-automation privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-10

robin-automation is used only by the owner of this site, on the owner's own Gmail account.

  • What it accesses: messages in the owner's Gmail inbox, through the Gmail API.
  • What it does: reads recent messages, adds labels, and archives job-application emails. It does not send or permanently delete mail.
  • Where data goes: nowhere. Processing happens on the owner's own computer. No email content is sold, shared, or sent to any third party.
  • Storage: the Gmail authorization is stored in the owner's macOS Keychain. Run logs record message IDs and label changes only.
  • Revoking access: the owner can remove access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

robin-automation's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
